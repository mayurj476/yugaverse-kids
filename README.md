# YugaVerse Kids 🐮🎈🔤

Seven gentle, colourful games for children around 2 years old: **shapes**, **balloons**, **ABC**, **animal sounds**, **colour pop**, **colouring** and a **talking cat**. They are made for small fingers that don't tap accurately yet. The animal, colour and colouring games speak **English, Hindi or Marathi**.

### ▶ Play: **https://mayurj476.github.io/yugaverse-kids/**

It works in any modern browser, and installs like an app on Android, iPad and iPhone. After the first visit it also works with no internet.

## Install it like an app

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| **Android** | Open the link in **Chrome**, tap **⋮** (top right), then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. |
| **iPad / iPhone** | Open the link in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. |
| **Computer** | Just open the link. In Chrome or Edge you can also click the install icon in the address bar. |

Once installed, it opens full screen from its own icon, with no browser bars.

## The games

The start screen has big picture cards, so a child who can't read can still choose.

### 🟡 Shapes
- Each level shows **3 random shapes** with smiling faces. There are 9 shapes in all, including circle, star, heart and moon.
- Each shape has a dashed outline in its own colour at the top. The child drags each shape into its outline.
- When all three are in, a **clapping cat** appears, with confetti and balloons the child can pop. Then the next level starts by itself.
- Each finished level adds a ⭐ at the top left.

### 🎈 Balloons
- Big balloons with the numbers **1 to 10** drift slowly in from every side.
- A tap near a balloon bursts it with a pop and a firework crackle. Colourful stars shoot out and sparks twinkle.
- The number grows out of the burst, and a voice says it ("Five!"). A short happy tune plays, higher for bigger numbers.
- Every 10 balloons, the clapping cat comes out to cheer.

### 🔤 ABC
- Two letters per level, in alphabet order: A and B, then C and D, up to Y and Z. After Z it starts again from A.
- The child drags each letter into its dashed outline. Picking a letter up makes the voice say it once.
- Placing it pops up a picture card, such as **"A for Apple 🍎"**, and the voice reads it out.
- After both letters are in, a **girl dances** to a bouncy song. Tapping her makes her laugh.
- The top bar shows how far the child has got (⭐ A–D).

### 🐮 Animal Sounds
- Eight soft cartoon animals on big cards: cow, dog, cat, duck, sheep, lion, elephant and pig.
- Tapping an animal makes its card bounce. The voice says the animal's name, then the animal makes its real sound.
- There is nothing to get wrong. Every tap is a good tap.

### 🥚 Color Pop
- One big glossy **egg or ball** at a time, in a random colour. It bounces in, and the voice says its colour once ("Red!", "लाल!").
- The first tap cracks it with a soft "tok". The second tap bursts it with a squishy splat. There's no firecracker sound.
- The paint splashes across the screen in its colour and drips. Each of the 3 lands in its own spot and stays until the party.
- After **3 bursts** the dancing girl comes out to celebrate, then it starts again.

### 🖌️ Coloring
- One big outline picture at a time, with a smiling face on most of them. There are 12: apple, fish, star, house, butterfly, cat, car, flower, sun, ice cream, duck and turtle. Each has 1 to 3 big parts to colour.
- **Six round paint bowls** are on the right: red, yellow, blue, green, orange and pink. Tapping a bowl dips the brush in it, and the voice says the colour.
- Tapping a part of the picture pours the paint in from the finger. Any colour is fine, and a part can be painted again in a new colour. Taps just next to a part still count.
- When every part is coloured, the picture jumps for joy and the **clapping cat** celebrates. Then a new picture comes.

### 🐱 Talking Cat
- A big cartoon cat sits on the grass. When the child talks, it tilts its head to listen, then **repeats what they said in a funny squeaky voice**, moving its mouth to the sound.
- **Tapping its head** makes it run across the screen and back with a real meow. **Tickling its belly** makes it giggle, and **tapping its tail** makes it jump and spin.
- Every few seconds it does something silly by itself, in random order: sticks its tongue out, winks, goes cross-eyed, puffs its cheeks, looks surprised, waves "Hi!" or dances with music notes. Now and then it invites the child to talk to it or tap it.
- **Microphone and privacy:** the browser asks once for microphone permission. The child's voice is only kept in the device's memory for a few seconds so it can be played back. It is never saved or uploaded. The microphone switches off as soon as the child leaves the game, or the app is minimised. The 🎤 at the top glows green while the cat is listening.
- Without a microphone (permission refused, or no mic), the cat still reacts to taps and does its silly faces.
- The microphone only works over **https** (the Play link) or on the computer running `run.bat`. Browsers block it on `http://<IP>:3000` from a tablet, so use the Play link on the iPad for this game.

## Parent settings
**Hold** the ⚙️ on the start screen for 2 seconds. A quick tap does nothing, so a toddler can't open it.
- **Language:** English, हिन्दी (Hindi) or मराठी (Marathi). This is used by Animal Sounds, Color Pop, Coloring and Talking Cat. The older games speak English for now.
- **Sounds** and **voice** can be switched on or off.
- **Reset** stars and letters.
- Settings say which voice your device will use. Many devices have no Marathi voice; then a Hindi voice reads the Marathi words. If there is no Hindi voice either, names are still shown on screen and the sounds still play.

## Made for little hands
- **Forgiving touch:** a tap *near* a shape, letter or balloon counts, even if it misses. Shapes and letters also snap in on their own once they get close to their outline. Several fingers at once work fine.
- **Every touch gets an answer:** tapping an empty spot makes a soft bloop and a few sparkles.
- **Gentle help:** if nothing happens for a few seconds, a 👆 hand shows where a piece goes, and the voice gives a hint.
- **Wrong drops are friendly:** the piece slides back with an "Oops! Try again", never a buzzer.
- **A natural voice:** the game picks the most natural-sounding voice on the device, and its lines change so it doesn't repeat itself. The voice always finishes before the cat claps or the girl dances.
- **Pauses by itself:** when the app is minimized, the screen locks, or another window is in front, everything stops (sound, voice, moving balloons). It carries on when the child comes back.
- **No accidental exits:** going back to the menu means **holding** 🏠 for about a second. A quick tap does nothing, and the Android back gesture can't close a game.
- **No ads, no accounts, no tracking.** Progress (stars and letters) is saved on the device only.

## Tips for parents
- **Better voice on iPad / iPhone:** go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, and download a voice marked **Enhanced** or **Premium** for English (such as *Ava (Premium)*) or Hindi. The game uses it automatically.
- **Hindi and Marathi on Android:** install the Hindi and Marathi voice data in Settings → *Text-to-speech output* (Google's speech engine).
- **Keep them in the game:** on iPad use *Guided Access* (Settings → Accessibility). On Android use *App pinning* (search for "pinning" in Settings).
- **Start over:** use **Reset** in the parent settings.

## Running it on your own computer
This section is for the owner and for anyone with permission (see [Licence](#licence)).

- **Quick look:** double-click `index.html`. Recorded sounds don't play this way.
- **On a tablet over home Wi-Fi:** double-click `run.bat`. It needs [Node.js](https://nodejs.org). A popup shows the address to type on the tablet, such as `http://192.168.1.14:3000`. Keep the black window open while playing. The first time, click **Allow** if Windows asks about the firewall. While it runs, the tablet reloads by itself whenever a file changes.

### Your own sounds
The game can make every sound by itself, but recordings replace them. Put `.mp3`, `.wav` or `.m4a` files in `sounds/`, with names such as `clap`, `applause`, `crackle`, `laugh`, `meow` or `dance`. Animal recordings go in `sounds/animals/`, named `cow`, `dog`, `cat`, `duck`, `sheep`, `lion`, `elephant` and `pig`. [sounds/README.txt](sounds/README.txt) lists every name. Silence at the start is skipped, loudness is evened out, and long recordings are trimmed with a soft fade.
